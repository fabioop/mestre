# HotKeyRecorder

An AppKit control 170 × 26 at `radius-field`: `panel-raised` fill, `hairline` border at 1 pt, or `accent-symbol` at 1.5 pt while recording. Text is `action` 13 pt centred: the combo in `ink`, or "Type shortcut…" in `ink-secondary`.

- Carbon hotkeys need no Accessibility permission; conflicts with Spotlight or Siri on ⌘Space are the user's to resolve, and the Welcome window says so.
- Don't: a clear button inside it, a dropdown of presets.
