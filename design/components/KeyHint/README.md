# KeyHint

A key combo in a cap: `key-cap` (11 pt medium, SF Rounded) on `key-cap` fill with a 1 px `key-cap-edge` border, 20 pt tall and at least 20 wide, padding 6, `radius-cap`. Glyph order ⌃⌥⇧⌘ then the key; Space and F-keys are words.

- In the footer it follows a `footer` word ("Open", "Actions") with a 5 pt gap; pairs are 12 pt apart. In the actions menu it sits alone at the trailing edge.
- The first action's cap is always ↩; the others show their binding or nothing (proposed: their ⌘number).
- Don't: write keys in prose, use the accent on it, put it on a result row.
